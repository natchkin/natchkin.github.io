Natchkin Period Pocket

Product Overview

Natchkin Period Pocket comes with a cotton-soft top layer that feels gentle on sensitive skin and doesn’t cause rashes, itching or irritation. The pads are VOC-free, phthalate-free and free from artificial fragrance, giving you clean and comfortable period care without unnecessary additives.

Compact, discreet and easy to carry anywhere, it fits effortlessly into your handbag, school bag, work bag or travel bag. With great absorbency and leak-proof protection, it keeps you protected while its lightweight, invisible feel makes it comfortable to wear through the day and night.
